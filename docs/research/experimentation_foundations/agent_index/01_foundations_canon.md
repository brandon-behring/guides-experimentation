## A2. Hypothesis-testing inference

- **Source:** **The ASA Statement on p-Values: Context, Process, and Purpose** — Wasserstein & Lazar (The American Statistician 2016). [10.1080/00031305.2016.1154108](https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2016.1154108) · [Berkeley free mirror (cached)](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Real_World/ASA_statement.pdf) · bibkey `wasserstein2016asa` · `ev_experimentation_foundations_0008`
  - **Mechanism:** American Statistical Association official statement issued in response to widespread p-value misuse and several journals' "banning" of p-values. Codifies six interpretive principles.
  - **Result:** Six principles: (1) p-values indicate incompatibility with a model; (2) they do NOT measure probability the hypothesis is true; (3) decisions should not rest on whether p crosses a threshold; (4) proper inference requires full reporting; (5) p does not measure effect size or importance; (6) p-value alone does not provide a good measure of evidence.
  - **Status:** `unverified` pending audit. Official ASA statement — `source_quality: official` in `evidence_ledger.yml`. Foundational for chapter 3 (hypothesis testing) of the pilot guide.
